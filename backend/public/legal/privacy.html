Hello Story — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 · Product path: /hello-story/privacy

This policy describes how Hello Story (“we”, “the App”) handles information when you use our interview, story, and video features.

Information we collect

  • Account: Sign in with Apple provides an Apple user identifier; we may store an optional email from Apple.
  • Your content: Interview answers, generated text, and video production data you create in the App.
  • Service data: Basic logs needed to operate and secure the service (e.g. request time, errors).

How we use information

  • Provide and improve interview, text, and video generation.
  • Maintain your account and stories across sessions.
  • Process content through AI services (language models, text-to-speech, image generation) to produce your outputs.

Third-party processors

We use third-party providers for AI, speech, and infrastructure. Your interview and production content may be sent to these providers only to deliver the features you request. Replace this section with your actual vendor list before App Store submission.

Retention & deletion

We retain your data while your account is active. You may delete your account in the App (Me → Delete account); we will remove associated server data subject to backup and legal limits.

Your rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information. Contact us via the Support page.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
